# Adoption Without Confidence — 2026 revision

This folder holds the public brief and carousel for the 2026 survey revision. The publication page is https://imaginationapplied.ai/publications/adoption-without-confidence/ . The analysis code, statistical tables and verification reports are maintained at https://github.com/jpenzell/ai-adoption-paradox .

The 2025 release remains frozen in `../adoption-without-confidence/` and archived under DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21896528 . That DOI does not identify this 2026 revision. No DOI has been assigned to this revision here.

The website stages these assets at explicit `-2026` download URLs, preserves all earlier download URLs, and labels the earlier material as the 2025 release. Source data are not redistributed. See the brief for survey attribution and limitations.
