# Adoption without confidence? Four-year edition (November 2026)

This folder holds the public four-year edition of the study, covering the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 Stack Overflow Developer Surveys. The publication page is https://imaginationapplied.ai/publications/adoption-without-confidence/ .

- `adoption-without-confidence-executive-brief.pdf`: four-page executive brief.
- `adoption-without-confidence-full-whitepaper.pdf`: complete 22-page whitepaper.
- `executive-brief-reading-edition.html`: reading edition of the brief.
- `report-reading-edition.html` and `report-reading-edition.md`: reading editions of the full whitepaper.

These files are published byte-identical to the approved versions; `MANIFEST.sha256` lists their hashes. The November 7, 2026 files remain in `../adoption-without-confidence-2026/` and the 2025 release remains frozen in `../adoption-without-confidence/`. Analysis code and verification evidence are maintained at https://github.com/jpenzell/ai-adoption-paradox . Source data are not redistributed.
